Privacy Policy

Effective 15 August 2026. Applies to grano.ca and related Grano brand sites. This is a working policy for a Canadian commercial site and should be reviewed by counsel before launch.

Who we are

Grano is a Canadian food brand. For privacy questions, write to hello@grano.ca with the subject line “Privacy”.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to run this site and answer you:

  • Name, email address, and message if you use the contact form
  • Optional consent to hear from us about stockists or wholesale (Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation)
  • A single local preference that records whether you dismissed the privacy notice
  • Standard server logs if the site is hosted on a platform that keeps them (IP address, browser, date), used to keep the site up and secure

We do not run advertising pixels, analytics suites, or cross-site tracking on this version of the site. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

How we use it

Contact details are used to reply to your enquiry, to keep a record of wholesale or press conversations, and, if you opted in, to send occasional email about where to buy Grano. We do not sell personal information. We do not use it to build lookalike audiences.

Legal basis (Canada)

We handle personal information under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where they apply, provincial private-sector privacy laws. Email marketing is sent only with consent as required by CASL. You can withdraw consent by emailing us.

Cookies and similar storage

This site stores one preference in your browser (localStorage) so the privacy notice stays dismissed. It is not used to identify you across sites. You can clear it by clearing site data in your browser.

How long we keep it

Contact form messages are kept as long as needed to handle the request and for a reasonable business record, then deleted. Opt-in email lists are kept until you unsubscribe or the list is closed.

Service providers

If we connect the contact form to an email or form provider, that provider will process the message in order to deliver it to us. Hosting providers may process server logs. We will only use providers we believe will handle the information carefully. Some may be outside Canada; if so, information may be subject to the laws of that country.

Your rights

You may ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we do not need to keep it. Write to hello@grano.ca. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

This site is aimed at adults buying or stocking food. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, we will update the date at the top of this page.
