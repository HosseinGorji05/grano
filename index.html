Questions

In the jar.

  • Each jar is built around chia mousse, oats, fruit, cacao, or almond. The full ingredient statement is on the pack. If you have an allergy, read the pack, not the website.

  • Keep jars refrigerated and eat them by the date on the lid. They are a chilled food. Once opened, eat the same day.

  • Because the jars are chilled, we do not treat them like shelf-stable e-commerce. If you want Grano in a shop, gym, or café, use the contact form and tell us the city.

  • The line includes tree nuts (almond, coconut), oats, and possible dairy depending on the base. The pack is the legal source of truth.

  • We are building retail and wholesale partners in Canada. Contact us with your city, or if you buy for a store, with a buyer email.

  • No. They are snacks and small meals around the day. We do not sell them as a diet plan.
